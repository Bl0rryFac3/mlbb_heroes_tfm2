# v2.7.0 — New hero Atlas, 26 heroes

## New hero · Atlas

Atlas joins as a melee Tank who sets up fights for his team. Frigid Breath slows nearby enemies and briefly freezes those who stay close. Annihilate sends out three bursts, with the separated suit providing a second origin. Perfect Match ejects the pilot and stuns nearby enemies when the suit rejoins. Fatal Links chains nearby enemy champions and pulls them toward Atlas. Using it while ejected rejoins the suit immediately.

Atlas has animated pilot and suit sprites, three skill icons, English cast audio for Annihilate and Fatal Links, and sound effects for ejection and reunion. Perfect Match has a 10-second cooldown and Fatal Links has a 34-second cooldown.

## Balance and fixes

- Granger’s Rhapsody deals **36 + 45% Attack** per round. Death Sonata deals **155 + 110% Attack**. His AI now preserves an active return-to-base order.
- Hanzo’s five-mark Demon Feast hits heroes harder. Hanekage’s Soul Snatch deals more damage, with **24% extra damage per mark against heroes**. Hanekage keeps **70% max HP**. Projection recovers in **26 seconds** after a normal return or **52 seconds** after defeat.
- Eudora’s first Forked Lightning hit deals **120 + 65% AP** against heroes. Thunder’s Wrath deals more damage at its center, outer ring and marked follow-up.
- Animated idle previews are restored when hovering **Atlas, Granger, Hanzo, and Popol & Kupa** in the match ban/pick grid.

## Install on Windows

Requires **Teamfight Manager 2 0.7.3 or newer**. Download **`mlbb_heroes_v2.7.0.zip`** under Assets. Close the game, move your previous `mlbb_heroes` folder outside `mods`, and extract the ZIP directly into `mods`. You should have `mods/mlbb_heroes/mod.mod_info`. Enable MLBB Heroes in the game and check that Mods shows **1.1.164**. The screenshot guide is inside the mod folder.
