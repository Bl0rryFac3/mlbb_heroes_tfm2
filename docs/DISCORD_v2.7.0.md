**MLBB Heroes v2.7.0 — Atlas joins the roster**

Atlas is hero 26. Eject from the suit with Perfect Match, then use Fatal Links to pull enemies in. Atlas has three skill icons and new in-match audio.

Granger, Hanzo and Eudora got damage buffs. Hanekage hits harder at five marks, and Atlas’s Perfect Match and Fatal Links now recover in **10s** and **34s**. The animated hover previews for Atlas, Granger, Hanzo and Popol & Kupa are back in match ban/pick.
