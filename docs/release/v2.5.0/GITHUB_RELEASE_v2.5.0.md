# v2.5.0 — New hero Granger, now 24 heroes

## New hero: Granger

- **The Death Chanter.** A ranged marksman with separate Gun and Cannon Energy, handgun barrages and explosive cannon rounds. Homing shots adapt his kit to TFM2's AI-controlled combat.
- **Capriccio (passive).** Both energy pools begin at *800*. Gun Energy regenerates at *8%* of maximum per second; Cannon Energy at *2%*. Lane-minion kills add *6* maximum Gun Energy and restore *5%*; jungle kills do the same for Cannon Energy. Champion kills and assists add *12* to both maxima and restore *10%*. Capacity growth persists through death.
- **Rhapsody (S1).** Fires *6* homing rounds over *1.5s*, each dealing *20 + 30% AD* physical damage and costing *70* Gun Energy. Requires more than *420* Gun Energy to begin. Granger gains *10% Movement Speed* while firing; each hit reduces Rondo's cooldown by *0.5s*. The final round gains *100% Critical Chance*.
- **Rondo (S2).** Dashes a short distance and restores *15%* of maximum Gun Energy. Walls and movement-disabling effects can stop the dash. Rondo can be used during Rhapsody.
- **Death Sonata (ultimate).** Costs *250* Cannon Energy and fires a homing cannon round at an enemy champion. Its impact deals *110 + 90% AD* physical damage to nearby enemies and slows them by *30%* for *0.6s*. Granger follows the shot with a short retreat roll. The cannon can also be fired during Rhapsody.
- **A complete visual and voice set for the adaptation.** Animated idle/run, handgun fire, dash, cannon, roll, hit and death states; distinct ordinary/final/cannon projectiles and impacts; official skill icons, colored descriptions and native champion portraits. Includes *19* mapped default-English voice events, with sparse movement chatter and shared match/pause/seek gating.

## Release notes

- **24 heroes · 8 language settings.** Granger and Popol & Kupa currently use English descriptions in every language setting; the other heroes retain their existing localized text.
- **Public release v2.5.0 · internal build 1.1.148.** The release uses the exact Windows DLL accepted by the owner. An already-playing voice clip may finish after pausing; the guard prevents new paused/menu voice starts and queued playback on resume.

Download **`mlbb_heroes_v2.5.0.zip`** from Assets below. Extract it, open the outer `mlbb_heroes_v2.5.0` folder and copy the inner `mlbb_heroes` folder into `Teamfight Manager 2\mods\` with the game closed. Replace the previous folder completely; do not merge versions. `Install_Guide.html` is beside the mod folder. If a prior loading failure disabled MLBB Heroes, re-enable it in the mod manager and restart if requested.

GitHub's automatic “Source code” archives are repository snapshots, not the installable mod. The matching development archive is **`MLBB_Heroes_TFM2_v1.1.148_v24_full_source_with_SDK_v2.5.0.zip`**. Seeing **1.1.148** in the mod manager is expected; **v2.5.0** is the public release label.
