# v2.4.0 — New hero Popol & Kupa, now 23 heroes

## New hero: Popol & Kupa

- **A marksman and his wolf companion.** Popol throws spears while Kupa closes in to bite. Damaging the same enemy builds up to *3* cooperative stacks, each granting both partners *5% Attack Speed* and *2% Movement Speed* for *4s*.
- **Bite ’em, Kupa! (S1).** Popol's spear deals *50 + 50% AD* physical damage, followed by Kupa's bite for *60 + 60% AD* and a *50%* slow for *1s*. In Alpha Wolf Form, the bite strikes nearby enemies for *90 + 75% AD* and knocks them airborne for *0.6s*.
- **Kupa, Help! (S2).** Calls Kupa back and grants Popol a *90 + 50% AD* shield for *3s*. When Kupa arrives, Popol rides him for up to *1.5s* with *50%* extra Movement Speed while Kupa recovers health. Alpha strengthens the shield and fully heals Kupa at mount start.
- **Popol’s Surprise (automatic traps).** During combat, Popol places a trap every *10s*, up to *2*. A triggered trap briefly immobilizes its victim and leaves *3s* of frost that slows enemies by *20%* and reduces Armor by *12%*. Traps are automatic to fit TFM2's three active skill slots.
- **We Are Angry! (ultimate).** Kupa fully recovers HP and transforms into Alpha Wolf Form for *9s*, gaining *35%* Max HP, enhanced skills and doubled cooperative bonuses.
- **Kupa can fall and return.** His health scales with levels and Popol's bonus Max HP, his defenses follow Popol's current stats, and he heals outside combat. Popol can complete an interruptible prayer to revive him. The duo has separate, mounted and Alpha animations, skill icons and colored ability text.

## Also in this release

- **23 heroes · 8 supported language settings.** The existing roster is joined by Popol & Kupa. Their descriptions currently use English fallback in all language settings; the other heroes retain their localized text.
- **Julian's voice playback respects the match being watched.** Setup, paused frames and replay catch-up cannot start a backlog of new lines.
- **Popol's introduction is included.** Additional movement, skill and death voice lines are not included in this release.
