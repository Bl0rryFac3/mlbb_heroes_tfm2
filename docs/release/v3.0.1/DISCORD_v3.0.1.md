🗡️ **MLBB Heroes — v3.0.1 · 30 heroes**

**New hero — Sun, the Monkey King.** A melee Fighter who can fight with two doppelgangers at once. Animated sprites and effects, official skill icons, colored tooltips, and his default English voice.

**Base stats** • 930 HP · 100 Attack · 32 Defense · 23 Magic Resist · 1,040 Move Speed
**Passive — Simian God.** Hits shred 5% Defense per stack, up to 30%. Clone hits heal Sun. Basics deal bonus damage to jungle camps.
**S1 — Endless Variety / Swift Exchange.** Throw the Golden Staff and summon a clone for up to 5s. A clear approach lets Sun travel with the staff and leave the clone behind.
**S2 — Instantaneous Move.** Sun and his clones rush one target, slow it, and hit enemies behind it.
**Ult — Clone Techniques.** Call a stronger 8s clone that can fight alongside the first.

**Fanny revamp — Blade Dancer.** 10 Steel Cable charges, midair redirects, wall and tower anchors, and escape cables when she retreats. Updated sprites, wires, hooks, Tornado Strike and Prey Marks. She follows her assigned lane or jungle role.
**Lesley** • Brighter muzzle flash and longer Ultimate Snipe tracer. The aim marker clears on interruption. Added her default English voice.
**Valir** • Added his default English voice, including Fireball, Torrent and Vengeance Flame.
**Roster** • Fanny’s 750 base HP now displays consistently. Belerick’s v3.0.0 base HP is 900.
