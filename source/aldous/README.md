# Aldous tuning notes

The 2026-10-23 candidate raises Soul Steal's Skill 1 stack coefficient to 2.5 raw damage per stack, capped at 1,000 (+2,500 at cap). At level 15, the primary hit is 2,762 raw damage before defense. It is benchmarked to one-shot a full-health level-15 Fanny under the standard defense relation, but TFM2 runtime behavior is not verified.

Aldous basic attack is restored to the original four frames at 0.065 s each, with action duration 14 ticks, hit tick 2, and 60-tick cooldown.
